About

How I think, what I bring to a team, and who I am beyond work.

Data with direction

I'm a people-first, data-driven problem solver. I turn ambiguity into clarity and help teams move ideas forward with structure, insight and intention.

I work where data, design and decision-making meet: insights become strategies, and strategies become stories that move people to act. Whether I'm managing a project, shaping a product or automating a workflow with AI, it starts from the same foundation. Know what the data says, know where it should take you, and bring people along.

My path runs from a computer science degree in India, to Amazon's global product teams, to a master's at Penn State, to a consulting firm where I've grown from product analyst to data scientist, strategy specialist and client project manager.

Product management, Penn State

PriorityPro: a feature prioritization assistant for product managers

Feature prioritization often comes down to whoever argues loudest. PriorityPro turns scattered feedback into scored, visual priorities that a team can discuss in one place.

I ran it as a full product lifecycle, from interviewing product managers about their needs through to testing and deployment. It's built in Adalo, with Tableau Public dashboards for prioritization scores and LiveChat for real-time collaboration.

  • Tableau Public
  • LiveChat
  • Heroku

Data science, Penn State

Which model should help decide a loan?

Loan approvals can be slow and subjective. Using 4,269 loan records, I compared three predictive models to find the most accurate, transparent way to support the decision.

The neural network was the most accurate at 95.75% on validation data. Credit score was the strongest single predictor, followed by the balance between income and loan size.

  • Python
  • IBM SPSS
  • Neural networks
  • Tableau
Read the loan prediction analysis on GitHub

Computer science, JNTU

Privacy-preserving profile publishing

Social platforms built on location data can reveal where people go. This web app lets people build social profiles and share activity while protecting their sensitive movement patterns.

Built in Java with JSP, servlets and MySQL on Apache Tomcat, it uses a heuristic publishing algorithm to share as much as possible within privacy limits, with separate admin and user experiences.

  • Java
  • JSP & Servlets
  • MySQL
  • Apache Tomcat
